Bionic Robotic Fish Platform — Brizo

Project Introduction Inspired by the streamlined body and efficient swimming patterns of the barracuda, I designed and developed the Brizo bionic robotic fish platform. I independently managed the entire development cycle, from conceptual sketching to a fully functional prototype. Measuring 0.5 meters in length, this platform integrates mechanical structures, embedded hardware, and advanced motion control algorithms, providing a comprehensive experimental platform for validating bionic propulsion and autonomous movement. Mechanical & Modeling: Designed the multi-joint bionic structure and streamlined hull using SolidWorks; constructed URDF models for simulation alignment.
